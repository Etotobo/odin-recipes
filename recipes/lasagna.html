Lasagna Recipe

Description

This is a simple lasagna recipe that everyone can make!
This Lasagna Recipe is easy to make with a creamy ricotta cheese mixture, savory meat sauce, and mozzarella cheese.

Ingredients

  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 pound bulk Italian sausage
  • 4 (15 ounce) cans tomato sauce
  • 1 (16 ounce) can sliced mushrooms, drained
  • 1 teaspoon garlic salt
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • ½ teaspoon dried thyme
  • ¼ teaspoon dried basil
  • salt and ground black pepper to taste
  • 1 (15 ounce) container ricotta cheese
  • 3 large eggs, beaten
  • ⅓ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 pound shredded mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
  2. Cook ground beef and sausage in a large pot over medium heat; drain.
  3. Meanwhile, bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil.
  4. Meanwhile, combine ricotta cheese, eggs, and Parmesan cheese in a bowl.
  5. Cover bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish with a thin layer of meat sauce.
  6. Bake in the preheated oven until cheese melts and sauce bubbles, about 90 minutes.
